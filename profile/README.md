## An open letter

Tyler Joseph found something at the kitchen sink. A song, a feeling, a reason. I took it as a picture: you're standing there doing something small and ordinary, you're deep in it, and then it clicks. Aha.

That's the whole idea of this organization. When you build something and you know what it's worth, you can feel it. Nobody else can tell you which turns you took to get there, the ones that were wrong, the ones that were late at night, the ones that made no sense until they did. Only you can explain that path, and that's where the purpose comes from.

The stuff we're building is what gives us purpose. This page is the collection of it.

If you got here, you're early. Look around.

— **Jedidiah Gabriel** ([Jedshock](https://jedshock.com))
